## Executive summary

Posture is solid for an rc: 28 findings, none critical, and the security lens produced no blocker — its two strongest items (unauthenticated control plane on 0.0.0.0, unauthenticated /query escrow growth) are recorded tradeoffs carrying accept-risk and defer, resting on operator-side controls that live outside this repo. Three findings block the milestone, all HIGH with recommend-fix: the release pipeline can publish a GHCR image from a commit whose unit tests failed, because .github/workflows/image.yml and ci.yml both fire on push to main with no dependency between them (OPS-F1); /v1/witness/loop reports a database failure for a telemetry-disabled configuration, sending an operator to the wrong component (DX-F1); and the seven control-plane GET routes shipped in 86848e4 are external contracts with no governing this.i node, which methodology section 10 makes a defect (CON-F1). The most urgent action is OPS-F1, because it is a small workflow change and it is the one finding that can put an untested artifact behind the rc tag. The remainder is ordinary hardening: three more container/CI supply-chain gaps (unfrozen uv sync, undigested base image, no HEALTHCHECK), two http-errors.md envelope omissions, and a maintainability cluster around undocumented seams and a hardcoded 16-byte slot stride. I merged nothing: two pairs look adjacent but are not the same defect — DX-F1 and MNT-F1 both misuse DbUnavailable, at reader.py:212 for missing telemetry and vitals.py:52 for a missing process, and they share a remedy (distinct error classes) without sharing a call site, so merging would hide the process case behind the loop case; likewise TST-F1 and TST-F5 both mention resident_bytes but test different layers, since test_metrics.py:59 stubs the reader with a literal dict while test_vitals.py:93 asserts only vm_rss_bytes.
